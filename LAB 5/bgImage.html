Terengganu formerly known as Tregganu or Tringganu,is a sultanate and constitutive state of federal Malaysia.The state is also know by its Arabic Honorific,Darul Iman ("Abode of Fait"). The coastal city of Kuala Terengganu which stands at the mount of the broad Terengganu River, is both the state and royal capital as well as the largest city in Terengganu.There are many islands located close to the coast of Terengganu state, such as Perhentian Islands and Redang Island.
