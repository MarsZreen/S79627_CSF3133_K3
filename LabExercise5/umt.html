About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a premier public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. Originally established as a fisheries and marine science center, it officially became a full-fledged university in 2007. UMT is widely recognized for its niche areas in marine science, aquatic resources, and oceanography.

The university is deeply committed to sustainable development and the conservation of marine ecosystems. Through rigorous academic programs and research facilities, UMT produces graduates who are highly skilled and environmentally conscious, fostering a vibrant student experience that balances academics with community engagement.
